## LANG 1402 - English for University Studies

- Course code: LANG 1402
- Title: English for University Studies
- Credits: 3 Credit(s)
- Common Core Area: E-Comm (English Communication)
- Prerequisite(s): (Level 3 in HKDSE English Language with all papers at or above level 3) OR (Level 4 in HKDSE English Language) OR (Level 5 in HKDSE English Language with some papers but not all at or above level 4) OR (Overall bandscore of 6.0 in IELTS) OR (Overall bandscore of 6.5 in IELTS with some but not all subscores at or above 6.0) OR equivalence of the above
- Exclusion(s): LANG 1002 (prior to 2022-23), LANG 1003 (prior to 2022-23), LANG 1401 (prior to 2024-25), LANG 1403, LANG 1404
- Corequisite(s): None listed
- Previous course code(s): CORE 1402
