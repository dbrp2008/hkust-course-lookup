## LANG 1403 - Academic English for University Studies

- Course code: LANG 1403
- Title: Academic English for University Studies
- Credits: 3 Credit(s)
- Common Core Area: E-Comm (English Communication)
- Prerequisite(s): LANG 1401 (prior to 2024-25) OR LANG 1402 OR Level 5 in HKDSE English Language with all papers at or above level 4 OR Overall bandscore of 6.5 in IELTS with all subscores at or above 6.0 OR equivalence of the above

- Corequisite(s): None listed
- Previous course code(s): CORE 1403
